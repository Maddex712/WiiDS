# WiiDS 1.4.2 - Pokémon XD: Gale of Darkness

Pokémon XD comes to WiiDS with dual screen menus, battle commands and the steel frame presentation.

- Party, Bag, Settings and battle commands appear on the bottom screen.
- Matching title-screen sky, area-tinted backgrounds and smoother battle transitions.
- The bottom fades to black through cutscenes and whole dialog exchanges, including speaker changes.
- Corrected battle directional inputs and removed the purple attack placeholder.
- Includes the slow area speed fix from v40.
- Added **WiiDS menu > Display > Resolution > 1080p (1920x1080)**. Existing choices and the default are unchanged; the choice saves automatically.

Audio has not yet been verified by ear. P★DA character mapping remains a known item.

Install **WiiDS-1.4.2-arm64.apk** over the existing Pokémon XD WiiDS app to keep saves and settings. This is the separate XD app; it does not replace the multi-game WiiDS 1.2.5 package. Supply your own supported USA revision 0 disc; no game data is included.
