# WiiDS 1.2

WiiDS 1.2 adds Mario Kart: Double Dash!!, Double Dash!! Plus, Super Smash Bros. Brawl, and Project+ to the dual-screen library on the AYN Thor. This is the signed ARM64 build 1200.

## New games and important fixes

- **Double Dash!! and Double Dash!! Plus** have separate cases, cards, settings, and backups. The lower display shows race standings, lap time, and course maps. The standard opening movie renders in color again.
- **Two-player controller routing** is fixed in both kart games. Each controller drives its own player. Balloon Battle and two-player split-screen Versus were tested on the Thor.
- **DS Cheep Cheep Beach** in Plus uses the course-authored minimap, with a moving player marker and a clean track outline.
- **Brawl and Project+** share the supported Brawl disc and use separate save profiles. Full matches in both reached the lower-screen Battle Records view. Project+ also showed stock losses and elimination.
- **Galaxy and Kaizo Rebalanced** include targeted fixes for texture and palette invalidation, framebuffer-copy ordering, and the color-combiner paths used by Lumas and Star Bits. The Thor passed sampled gameplay and focused GPU checks.
- **Texture Pack Store** has separate selections for all 12 cases. Actual replacements were tested on Galaxy, Kaizo, Double Dash and Plus.

## Install

Download **WiiDS-1.2-arm64.apk** from this release, or open **Options → Check for updates** in WiiDS. Install over the current app to retain your existing data. You can export saves in **Options → Save manager** before updating.

Import your own supported retail discs. Double Dash uses USA **GM4E01 revision 0** and Brawl uses USA **RSBE01 revision 2**. To install Plus, open **Options → Your games → Double Dash Plus** and select the creator’s **Plus 3.3 NTSC xdelta** after setting up standard Double Dash. Plus and the original use independent memory cards. Project+ uses its own case behind Brawl and can migrate an existing WiiDS Project+ setup. [Double Dash Plus patch topic](https://retroachievements.org/forums/topic/27182)

## Thor verification and known limits

- Finished all seven laps of a Baby Park Time Trial and reached the post-race menu. Mirror Grand Prix on Luigi Circuit showed reversed gameplay, live standings, and pause. The lower-screen map is hidden in Mirror mode, and Battle shows teams and the timer without an arena map.
- The P1 map label can overlap the Baby Park title near the top edge. Grand Prix finishes and physical secondary USB or Bluetooth controllers were outside this pass. The two-player checks used the Thor controls and a second Android test controller.
- Plus Save Manager export and restore passed byte-for-byte. The separate standard card matched after the tests.
- The specific community-reported white Luma, Grand Star, and flickering-surface scenes lack direct live confirmation. The renderer fixes address the paths covered by the tests.
- Project+ and Double Dash Plus case art is sourced from the listings below. Creator attribution is unverified. [Project+ cover listing](https://thegamesdb.net/game.php?id=125563) · [Double Dash Plus cover listing](https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com/games/images/407008-mario-kart-double-dash-plus)
