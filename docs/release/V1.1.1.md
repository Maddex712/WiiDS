# WiiDS 1.1.1

WiiDS 1.1.1 brings Retro Rewind 6.13.4 with online play, custom GPU drivers, Lossless Scaling frame generation, rumble on the AYN Thor, a Wii Menu theme, and a set of bug fixes reported on GitHub and Discord.

**Updating from 1.1:** open **Options > Check for updates** in WiiDS, or download `WiiDS-1.1.1-arm64.apk` below and install it over your current copy. **Do not uninstall or clear app data.** Your imported games, saves and settings stay in place. The download is the full APK (about 1.8 GB); allow about 3.7 GiB of free storage.

## New

### Lossless Scaling frame generation (Mario Kart Wii and Retro Rewind)

- **Options > Frame generation** chooses between the built-in interpolation and Lossless Scaling.
- Lossless Scaling needs your own `Lossless.dll` from **Lossless Scaling on Steam** (it is in the Lossless Scaling folder of your Steam library). Import it from the same page. WiiDS does not include it and never downloads it.
- In a race, set **Frame rate > 120 FPS (Lossless Scaling)**. The Display settings show "Lossless Scaling: active" while it is generating frames, or the reason it is not. If it cannot run, it turns itself off and the game continues with repeated frames; it never closes the game.
- Frame generation runs on the GPU through [Bloodframe](https://github.com/ducvd89/bloodframe) (MIT), with a WiiDS patch that falls back to a copy mode on devices whose graphics allocator cannot share storage images (such as the Thor).

### Rumble

- **Mario Kart Wii, Retro Rewind, New Super Mario Bros. Wii, MSMBWii, Super Mario Sunshine, Super Mario Eclipse, Super Mario Galaxy and Kaizo Mario Galaxy** now rumble.
- The Thor's built-in controls have no motor of their own, so their rumble plays on the Thor's vibration motor. Controllers with their own motors rumble themselves; in Sunshine and Eclipse this works for the first time.
- **Android's Vibration setting must be on** (Settings > Sound & vibration > Vibration & haptics). Android blocks all game rumble while it is off, and apps cannot read that setting.
- **Options > Rumble** has a **Test rumble** button and a shortcut to Android's vibration settings. If the test does nothing, Vibration is off.
- NSMB and MSMBWii's in-game **Controller vibration** toggle now works.

### Custom GPU drivers (Mario Kart Wii and Retro Rewind)

- **Graphics > GPU Driver** installs a Turnip or Adreno driver ZIP and selects it per device. The game falls back to the system driver if a driver cannot load.
- Driver loading uses [libadrenotools](https://github.com/bylaws/libadrenotools) (BSD-2-Clause).

### Menu themes

- **Options > Menu Theme** switches the launcher, dialogs and lower screen between the Mario Kart Wii look and a Wii Menu look.

## Retro Rewind

- **Updated to Retro Rewind 6.13.4.**
- **Retro WFC online is now included.** The previous build had no online client.
- **Updater:** it now updates straight through a release WiiDS cannot run (6.13.3) to the next one it can (6.13.4), instead of stopping. It accepts the official expert ghost folders, and keeps your own ghosts and My Stuff.
- The frame rate fix works on 6.13.4.

## Fixes

- **NSMB and MSMBWii:** tilt goes in the right direction, and unlocked worlds show on the lower-screen world map.
- **GameCube disc import:** imported discs are validated more carefully.
- **"Import again"** opens the file picker, including for files on a microSD card.
- **Super Mario Galaxy and Kaizo:** a disc chosen in any file app is copied in directly, so it no longer has to be copied to the device first. This also works when WiiDS does not have All files access.
- **GPU driver page:** installing and saving a driver works (GitHub #4); it no longer fails with "Could not save".
- **Galaxy:** the prologue movies no longer show corrupted (flat green) frames (GitHub #1). Spin works with each assigned button, and Quick Save saves again (GitHub #7).

## Testing

Tested on an AYN Thor (Adreno 740):

- Mario Kart Wii boots and runs with Lossless Scaling active (one generated frame per game frame, 120 Hz), checked on the title screen.
- Retro Rewind 6.13.4 installed through the in-app updater, booted, and completed a VS race.
- With a custom driver package selected, Mario Kart Wii and Retro Rewind loaded it and rendered their menus.
- A Galaxy disc chosen through Android's file picker imported (with cutscene movies) and Galaxy booted.
- The NSMB "Import again" picker imported a disc from a microSD card.
- Options > Rumble's test buzz reaches Android's vibrator as media vibration.

Not yet tested on hardware:

- **Retro WFC online.** Connecting registers a profile on your Wii license.
- **Lossless Scaling in an actual race**, and on devices other than the Thor.
- **Rumble felt during gameplay** in each game.
- **NSMB tilt direction**, which needs a physical tilt check.
